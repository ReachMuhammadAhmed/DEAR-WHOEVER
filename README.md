Mail It Forward is a tiny art exchange that makes the internet feel
hand-delivered again. You draw a postcard on a 32×32 pixel canvas —
small enough that anyone can make art, big enough to say something —
write a note to a stranger, pick a stamp, and mail it. Somewhere in
the world, someone receives your one-of-one original and mails one of
their own forward. No feeds, no likes, no algorithms: just a mailbox
full of tiny kindnesses from people you'll never meet. Built for the
"Make the Internet Fun" theme, because the best part of the internet
was always finding something a human made just for you.
